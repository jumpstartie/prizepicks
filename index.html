    How the value score works

    • Edge (projection vs. posted line) is the primary driver.
    • Softer opponent matchups add a bounded bonus.
    • Recent form (L10) agreeing with the lean adds confidence.
    • Scores of 70+ are High confidence, 50-69 Medium.

    Slip discipline

    • Prefer High-confidence legs; each added leg raises variance.
    • Diversify across games so one rainout can't sink the slip.
    • Fade Unders on strikeout props against cold offenses.
